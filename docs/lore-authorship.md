# CaRL — Lore & Authorship

**Status:** Working continuity note for Milestone 7, 14 October 2026. This file records what is established, what was changed, and what is intentionally unresolved.

## Timeline

| Date | Status | Event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1878 | Established CaRL story setting | Carl follows a luminous Orb into a St. Roch Cemetery mausoleum in New Orleans. The Demon and the Rune are part of the adventure. |
| 2026 | Approved CaRL narrator date | Dark4nal0g, described as a programmer in New Orleans, tells Carl's story through the Chronolith Adventure Runic Labyrinth Simulator. |
| 2101 | Existing wider-lore provenance reference, **not retconned here** | Historical project notes also use 2101 in describing C.A.R.L. simulator provenance. Determine how that record relates to the 2026 narrator only if/when the broader Terminal Lore continuity is updated. |

These years do **not** mean Carl lives in 2026. The game itself remains set in 1878.

## Names and attribution

- **Steelwater:** real-world creator credit for the CaRL game. This is an out-of-world credit, not the name of an in-world programmer.
- **Dark4nal0g:** in-world programmer/narrator, described as based in New Orleans, 2026. Preserve this handle in the game's About screen.
- **One Touched:** a legacy attribution appearing in CaRL's pre-Milestone-7 README for the creation/preservation of the simulator. Whether this is an alias, distinct figure, title, institution or superseded draft is **undecided**. Do not equate One Touched with Dark4nal0g or Steelwater without an explicit story decision.
- **Carl:** boy whose adventure takes place in St. Roch Cemetery in 1878.
- **Chronolith Adventure Runic Labyrinth Simulator:** fictional simulator through which the story is told.
- **Runic Labyrinth / Terminal Lore:** related fiction and projects, but their chronologies and experimental mechanics must not be silently merged into CaRL.

## Player-facing presentation

- **Story (desktop and mobile):** retain the 1878 story hook, Carl and the Orb. Do not use it to present real-world authorship.
- **About (desktop and mobile):** identify C.A.R.L. Simulator, the 2026 fictional narrator Dark4nal0g, 1878 recorded adventure, and Steelwater as creator.
- **Gameplay Log:** retain chronological run events, not a static authorship essay.
- **README:** explicitly separate the 1878 event, 2026 fictional narrator and Steelwater credit. Preserve mention of One Touched as a continuity question here rather than claim a definitive identity.

## Boundaries for future edits

1. Do not globally replace "2101" in other Terminal Lore materials.
2. Do not replace Dark4nal0g with Steelwater in fiction.
3. Do not invent a relationship between One Touched and Dark4nal0g; mark it unresolved.
4. Avoid changing gameplay, secret inputs, Orb rules, controls, save data or the final Rune event during this lore-only milestone.
5. Leave the Playtiles prototype and its milestone on hold while awaiting a response; no Playtiles integration is authorized.
