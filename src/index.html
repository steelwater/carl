St. Roch Cemetery, New Orleans, 1878. In the long age of ghouls, ghosts, fever, and mourning, a boy named Carl follows a luminous Orb into a sealed cemetery mausoleum.

          
Dark4nal0g a programmer in New Orleans, 2101, tells the story of Carl through the Chronolith Adventure Runic Labyrinth Simulator.

        

        

          
Goal
Guide Carl through three Mausoleum depths, defeat the Demon, let the Orb absorb the glowing Rune, then return to the Cemetery.

          
Turns & Movement
Movement and Orb activation advance the simulation. Carl moves one tile normally and up to two tiles while powered. Ghouls move after Carl.

          
The Orb
. grants 20% XP. At 100% XP, the Orb levels up and gains 2 Skill Points. O grants 1 Light Aura charge.

          
Cemetery Recovery
Retreating above ground under the Full Moon restores Light Auras to 2 and completely refills the Defensive Aura.

          
Defensive Aura
Damage drains the Aura before Carl loses HP. Press E to spend 1 Skill Point and increase its maximum strength by 5.

          
Light Aura
Press Space to consume 1 charge. Carl becomes C, moves faster, causes ghouls to flee, destroys them by contact, and can damage the Demon. Press Q to make the light last longer and strike harder.

          
Exploration
Find k before entering M. Use < to retreat upward. Explored areas remain mapped, while creatures and items are only shown in Carl’s current line of sight.

          
Final Objective
Defeat D, collect the glowing R, and climb back to the Cemetery.
